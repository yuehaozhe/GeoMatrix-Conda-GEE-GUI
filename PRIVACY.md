GeoMatrix Studio Privacy Policy
Effective Date: September 15, 2026
Operator: Haozhe Yue / GeoMatrix Studio (Contact: contact@geomatrix.dev)

I. Our Promise
GeoMatrix Studio is built local-first for researchers. Your geospatial data, Conda environments, Google credentials, and compute results stay on your device by default.
We do not sell your data. We do not run ads. We do not embed behavioral analytics, usage profiling, or hidden sync that phones home with your research.
Earth Engine and GCP traffic goes from your machine straight to Google over HTTPS — not through a GeoMatrix Studio relay.

II. What We Do Not Collect
- Your research datasets, project files, or local computation outputs
- Google passwords, or clickstream / advertising profiles
- Automatic crash uploads or product-usage telemetry (logs remain local unless you choose to export and share them)

III. What Little We Touch
To keep the beta safe and up to date, the app may perform a lightweight version check against update.geomatrix.dev (fetching a public latest.json). That request is for release metadata only — not your files, credentials, or research content.
During this closed beta, no paid checkout and no license activation are required.

IV. Services You Choose to Use
When you sign in to Google, install packages, or open a feedback channel, your device talks directly to that provider. Those requests follow their policies, not ours:
- Google / Earth Engine / GCP
- Package sources such as Micromamba, Conda-Forge, PyPI, and PyTorch
- Feedback you send via GitHub, email, X, or similar

V. On Your Device
Settings, local GEE task history, environment templates, and app logs may be stored in the application data folder. Removing the app data directory clears them (paths differ by OS).

VI. Security & Transparency
Connections use HTTPS. We have no remote channel to read research files off your disk. Network endpoints used by the product are documented for audit (including packet-capture friendly inspection).

VII. Your Choices
- Skip Google / cloud features entirely if you prefer fully offline conda workflows
- Export logs locally and decide whether to send them to us
- Manage update preferences in Settings when the control is available

VIII. Contact
Privacy questions: contact@geomatrix.dev

IX. Updates
We may revise this policy and publish a new effective date. Material changes may be noted on our website, social channels, or in-app notices.
